## Ingredients

- 680 g tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- 5 slices ginger
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 60 ml water, for the slurry
- 30 ml cooking oil
- 45 ml water, for the tomatoes
- 0.5 tsp salt, for the eggs
- 0.5 tsp salt, for the tomatoes
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 30 ml ketchup

## Method

### 1. Peel and cut the tomatoes

Peeling is optional. To peel, score an X into each tomato and cover with boiling water for about 2 minutes, until the skin begins to curl. Lift out and remove the skins. Cut out the stem ends and cut the tomatoes into wedges.

### 2. Prepare the ginger

Slice the ginger thinly, then cut it into fine strips.

### 3. Beat the eggs

Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the salt, and beat vigorously until combined and airy.

### 4. Mix the slurry

Stir the cornstarch and the water for the slurry together in a small bowl.

### 5. Scramble the eggs

Heat a pan over high heat until hot but not smoking. Add some of the oil and swirl to coat. Pour in the eggs and gently scramble. When about 80% set, after roughly 35 seconds, transfer to a plate.

### 6. Cook the tomatoes

Add the remaining oil and ginger to the pan, then add the tomatoes. Stir-fry for about 30 seconds. Add the water for the tomatoes, the salt, and brown sugar. Cook for about 2½ minutes, covering if needed to maintain a simmer. Stir the slurry and add gradually until the sauce thickens to your liking. Return the eggs and cook for 50 seconds. Turn off the heat, add the ketchup, and mix.

### 7. Taste and serve

Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed, then transfer to a serving dish.
