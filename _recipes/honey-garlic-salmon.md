## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 60 ml honey
- 30 ml soy sauce
- 15 ml white vinegar
- 1 large clove garlic, minced

### Salmon

- 2 salmon fillets, about 200 g each
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Black pepper

### Optional garnish

- Sesame seeds
- Chives or spring onions, finely sliced

## Method

### 1. Season the salmon

If time allows, take the salmon out of the refrigerator about 20 minutes before cooking. Pat it dry and season with salt and black pepper.

### 2. Mix the sauce

Whisk together the honey, soy sauce, vinegar, and garlic in a small bowl.

### 3. Sear the salmon

Heat a little olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Cook the salmon for 3–4 minutes on the first side until golden. Turn and cook for another 2–3 minutes.

### 4. Glaze the salmon

Pour the sauce into the pan and cook for about 1 minute, turning the salmon to coat it as the sauce thickens. If the sauce thickens before the fish is cooked to your liking, add water 15 ml at a time.

### 5. Serve

Transfer the salmon to plates and spoon over the pan sauce. Finish with sesame seeds and sliced chives or spring onions, if desired.
