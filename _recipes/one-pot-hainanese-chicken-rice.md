## Ingredients

### Chicken rice

- 400 g jasmine rice
- 2 spring onions, trimmed and halved
- 3 slices fresh ginger
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 625 ml chicken stock
- 4 chicken drumsticks
- 4 small skin-on, boneless chicken thigh fillets

### Spring onion sauce

- 100 g spring onions, finely sliced
- 5 g sea salt
- 60 ml vegetable oil
- 10 ml sesame oil

### To serve

- Sambal oelek
- Cucumber, sliced

## Method

### 1. Cook the chicken and rice

Rinse the rice. Put it in the rice cooker with the spring onions, ginger, garlic, and stock. Nestle in the drumsticks and thigh fillets. Cook on the rice cooker's automatic setting until the cycle finishes and the chicken is cooked through.

### 2. Make the spring onion sauce

Pound the sliced spring onions with the salt until coarsely crushed. Heat the vegetable and sesame oils in a small saucepan until shimmering, then carefully pour them over the spring onions and stir.

### 3. Serve

Slice the thigh fillets and serve the chicken with the rice, spring onion sauce, sambal oelek, and cucumber.
