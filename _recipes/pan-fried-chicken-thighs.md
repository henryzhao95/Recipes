## Ingredients

### Chicken and aromatics

- 480 g chicken thighs
- ½ red onion
- ½ white onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 24 g cornstarch, for coating
- 30 ml cooking oil

### Marinade

- 15 ml oyster sauce
- 15 ml light soy sauce
- 15 ml cooking wine
- 5 g salt
- 4 g sugar
- 1 g white pepper
- 16 g cornstarch
- 14 g fresh ginger

### To finish

- 15 ml cooking wine
- 2.5 g garlic salt

## Method

### 1. Make the marinade

Mix the oyster sauce, soy sauce, cooking wine, salt, sugar, white pepper, and cornstarch in a bowl. Mince the ginger, add it, and stir until combined.

### 2. Prepare the aromatics

Slice the red and white onions. Mince the garlic.

### 3. Prepare the chicken

Trim excess fat if desired. Debone the thighs if needed, then cut into even thickness so they cook consistently.

### 4. Marinate and coat

Pat the chicken dry and coat it in the marinade. Leave for about 10 minutes. Spread the coating cornstarch on a plate and lightly coat both sides of each thigh; shake off excess.

### 5. Pan-fry the chicken

Heat the oil in a skillet or wok. Reduce the heat to medium-low and place the chicken skin-side down. Cook for 2–3 minutes until golden, turn and cook for 5–6 minutes, then return it skin-side down for another 1–2 minutes to crisp.

### 6. Finish with aromatics

Move the chicken to the sides of the pan. Add the garlic to the centre and cook for about 10 seconds, then add the onions and cook for 20 seconds. Pour in the cooking wine and cover immediately. Cook covered over high heat for 30–40 seconds. Uncover, season with garlic salt, and serve the chicken over the onions.
