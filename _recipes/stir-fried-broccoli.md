## Ingredients

### Broccoli

- 340 g broccoli
- 10 g salt, divided
- Water, for soaking and cooking

### White sauce

- 2.5 g salt
- 4 g sugar
- 5 ml cooking wine
- 5 g chicken bouillon
- 3 g cornstarch
- 30 ml water

### Water and wine

- 120 ml water
- 15 ml cooking wine

### Stir-fry

- 30 ml corn oil

## Method

### 1. Wash and soak the broccoli

Dissolve 10 g salt in a basin of water. Soak the broccoli florets-side down for 2–3 minutes, moving them gently to dislodge dirt. Rinse and drain.

### 2. Mix the white sauce

Stir together the remaining salt, sugar, cooking wine, chicken bouillon, cornstarch, and 30 ml water until the starch dissolves.

### 3. Mix the water and wine

Combine 120 ml water with 15 ml cooking wine and keep it beside the stove.

### 4. Cut the broccoli

Trim the broccoli. Cut through the stems without slicing through the florets, then split any large pieces through the stem so they are similar in size.

### 5. Stir-fry and simmer

Heat a wok over high heat. Add the oil and heat until shimmering, then lower to medium and add the broccoli. Toss briefly, raise the heat to high, and stir-fry for 20–30 seconds. Add the water-wine mixture and cover promptly. Cook covered for 4 minutes. Uncover, reduce the heat, and add the white sauce. Toss over high heat for 20–30 seconds, then serve.
