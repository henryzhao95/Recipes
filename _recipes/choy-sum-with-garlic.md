## Ingredients

- 450 g choy sum
- A small piece of ginger, about 4 thin slices
- 5 cloves garlic
- 30 ml cooking oil, divided
- 5 ml cooking wine
- 4 g sugar
- 2.5 g salt

## Method

### 1. Prepare the choy sum

Wash and trim the choy sum. Separate the leaves from the thicker stems, then cut the stems into manageable lengths.

### 2. Prepare the aromatics

Slice two garlic cloves and mince the remaining three. Cut the ginger into thin slices.

### 3. Fry the aromatics

Heat a wok over high heat. Add 15 ml of the oil, reduce the heat to medium-low, then stir-fry the ginger and garlic for about 15 seconds until fragrant.

### 4. Stir-fry the greens

Add the stems and stir-fry for about 30 seconds. Add the leaves, raise the heat to high, then add the cooking wine and sugar. Toss for another 30 seconds.

### 5. Steam and season

Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover, and steam for 1½–2 minutes until just tender. Uncover and stir-fry on high for about 1 minute. Add the salt and remaining oil, toss for 20 seconds, then serve.
