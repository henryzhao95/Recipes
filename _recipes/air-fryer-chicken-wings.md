## Ingredients

- Cooking spray
- 680 g chicken wings, split into flats and drumettes
- 15 ml olive oil
- 1 tsp paprika
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 0.5 tsp onion powder
- 0.75 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Method

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><video controls playsinline preload="metadata" poster="https://content.jwplatform.com/thumbs/7IDLEPL5-720.jpg" aria-label="Air Fryer Chicken Wings"><source src="https://content.jwplatform.com/videos/7IDLEPL5.mp4"><a href="https://content.jwplatform.com/videos/7IDLEPL5.mp4">Watch Air Fryer Chicken Wings</a></video></div>

1. Pat the wings dry with paper towels and transfer them to a large bowl.

   [![Chicken wings in a bowl with seasonings and oil added on top.](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-02.jpg)](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-02.jpg)

2. Add the olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Toss until each wing is evenly coated.

   [![Chicken wings tossed with oil and seasoning until coated.](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-03.jpg)](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-03.jpg)

3. Preheat the air fryer to 195°C and lightly coat the basket with cooking spray. Arrange the wings skin-side down in a single layer with space between them; cook in batches if needed.

   [![Seasoned chicken wings arranged in an air fryer basket.](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-04.jpg)](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-04.jpg)

4. Cook for 10 minutes, flip the wings, raise the temperature to 205°C, then cook for another 10–13 minutes until crisp and golden.

   [![Crispy chicken wings after air frying.](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-05.jpg)](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Air-Fryer-Chicken-Wings-05.jpg)

5. Serve with celery and ranch dressing, if desired.
