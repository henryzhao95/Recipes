## Ingredients

- Cooking spray
- 680 g chicken wings, split into flats and drumettes
- 15 ml olive oil
- 2 g paprika
- 1 g garlic powder
- 1 g onion powder
- 4 g salt
- 1 g black pepper

## Method

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><video controls playsinline preload="metadata" poster="https://content.jwplatform.com/thumbs/7IDLEPL5-720.jpg" aria-label="Air Fryer Chicken Wings"><source src="https://content.jwplatform.com/videos/7IDLEPL5.mp4"><a href="https://content.jwplatform.com/videos/7IDLEPL5.mp4">Watch Air Fryer Chicken Wings</a></video></div>

1. Preheat the air fryer to 195°C and lightly coat the basket with cooking spray.
2. Pat the wings dry, then toss with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Arrange the wings in a single layer, skin-side down. Cook for 10 minutes, flip, raise the temperature to 205°C, and cook for another 10–13 minutes until crisp and cooked through.
4. Serve with celery and ranch dressing, if desired.
