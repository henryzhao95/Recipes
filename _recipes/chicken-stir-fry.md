## Ingredients

### Main

- 450 g broccoli
- 340 g chicken breast
- 2 cloves garlic
- 14 g ginger
- 28 g green onion whites
- 28 g carrot
- 1.9 L blanching water
- 1 tsp salt
- 10 ml oil, for blanching
- 30 ml oil, for stir-frying
- 5 ml cooking wine
- 0.5 tsp sugar
- 5 ml sesame oil

### Marinade

- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 30 ml water
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 15 ml oyster sauce
- 0.25 tsp white pepper

### Stir-fry sauce

- 15 ml oyster sauce
- 15 ml light soy sauce
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 1.5 tsp sugar
- 10 ml cooking wine
- 0.25 tsp white pepper

### Slurry

- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 30 ml water

## Method

### 1. Prepare the broccoli

Soak and rinse the broccoli. Separate the florets, peel the tough outer layer from the stems, and slice the stems thinly. Rinse the cut broccoli and drain.

### 2. Slice and marinate the chicken

Slice the chicken breast thinly. Mix the cornstarch, water, salt, oyster sauce, and white pepper until smooth. Coat the chicken in the marinade and set aside.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/b1v0HeekO5Y?start=35&amp;end=197" title="Prep chicken — Chicken Stir-Fry with Broccoli" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 3. Prepare the aromatics

Mince the garlic. Peel and slice the ginger, cut the green onion whites into short lengths, and slice the carrot thinly on the diagonal.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/b1v0HeekO5Y?start=197&amp;end=366" title="Prep aromatics — Chicken Stir-Fry with Broccoli" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 4. Mix the stir-fry sauce

Combine the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, salt, sugar, cooking wine, and white pepper in a small bowl.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/b1v0HeekO5Y?start=366&amp;end=391" title="Prep sauce — Chicken Stir-Fry with Broccoli" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 5. Mix the slurry

Stir the cornstarch and water together until combined.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/b1v0HeekO5Y?start=391&amp;end=399" title="Prep slurry — Chicken Stir-Fry with Broccoli" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 6. Blanch the broccoli

Bring the blanching water to a rolling boil. Add the salt and oil, then blanch the broccoli for 4–5 minutes. When the water returns to a boil, transfer the broccoli to ice water for 1 minute; drain.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/b1v0HeekO5Y?start=399&amp;end=541" title="Blanch broccoli — Chicken Stir-Fry with Broccoli" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 7. Stir-fry the chicken

Heat a wok over high heat until hot. Add the oil and lower the heat. Let the chicken brown on one side for about 30 seconds, then stir-fry until cooked, about 2 minutes. Remove from the wok.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/b1v0HeekO5Y?start=541&amp;end=639" title="Stir-fry chicken — Chicken Stir-Fry with Broccoli" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 8. Finish the stir-fry

Stir-fry the garlic, ginger, green onion, and carrot for 30–40 seconds. Add the broccoli and a splash of cooking wine; stir-fry for 1½–2 minutes. Return the chicken to the wok for 30 seconds.

Stir the sauce, pour it into the wok, and toss for 15–20 seconds. Add the slurry gradually while stirring until the sauce reaches the desired thickness. Finish with sesame oil, then turn off the heat and serve.
