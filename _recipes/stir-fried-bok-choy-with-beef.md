## Ingredients

### Main

- 450 g bok choy
- 340 g flank steak
- 14 g carrot
- 3 cloves garlic, divided
- 14 g fresh ginger
- 2 green onions

### Beef marinade

- 15 ml oyster sauce
- 10 ml light soy sauce
- 15 ml rice wine
- 30 ml water
- 0.25 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 tbsp cornstarch

### Beef sauce

- 15 ml oyster sauce
- 10 ml light soy sauce
- 2.5 ml dark soy sauce
- 10 ml rice wine
- 1 tsp sugar
- White pepper, a pinch
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon, optional
- 15 ml oil

### Slurry

- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 15 ml water

### Stir-fry

- 30 ml oil, divided
- 5 ml cooking wine
- 30 ml water, plus 15 ml if needed
- 0.5 tsp sugar
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 5 ml sesame oil

## Method

### 1. Wash the bok choy

Soak the bok choy in a large bowl of water for 10 minutes. Remove the outer leaves and rinse away dirt, then drain with the stems facing down.

### 2. Prepare the beef

Trim the silver skin from the flank steak. Slice thinly against the grain, then rinse in cold water for 2 minutes and drain.

### 3. Mix the marinade

Stir together the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, rice wine, water, white pepper, sugar, baking soda, and cornstarch until smooth.

### 4. Marinate the beef

Squeeze excess water from the beef. Mix it with the marinade for 2 minutes, then set aside while preparing the remaining ingredients.

### 5. Prepare the aromatics

Mince the garlic. Slice the ginger and carrot thinly. Separate the green onion whites and halve them.

### 6. Cut the bok choy

Depending on size, halve or quarter the bok choy so the pieces cook evenly.

### 7. Mix the sauce and slurry

Combine the oyster sauce, both soy sauces, rice wine, sugar, white pepper, and bouillon if using. In a separate bowl, mix the cornstarch and water for the slurry. Stir half of the stir-fry oil into the marinated beef.

### 8. Stir-fry the bok choy

Heat half of the oil in a wok over high heat. Add half the garlic and stir-fry for 30 seconds. Add the bok choy and toss for 1–2 minutes. Add the cooking wine, cook for 20 seconds, then add the water. Cover and steam on low for 40–50 seconds. Uncover, add the sugar and salt, toss, then transfer the bok choy to a plate and rinse the wok.

### 9. Stir-fry the beef

Heat the remaining oil in the wok. Add the ginger, carrot, green onion whites, and remaining garlic; stir-fry for 1 minute. Add the beef and spread it out. Cook for 1 minute, turn the pieces, then add the sauce. Stir in slurry as needed to thicken. Return the bok choy and toss for 30 seconds. Finish with sesame oil and serve.
