## Ingredients

- About 450 g Chinese broccoli
- 1.9 L boiling water
- 15 ml oyster sauce
- 15 ml olive oil
- About 1 tsp salt

## Method

### 1. Wash the gai lan and heat the water

Rinse the Chinese broccoli well. Bring a generous amount of water to a boil in a wok or large pot.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/zi3FB2NtLCc?start=58&amp;end=92" title="Wash gai lan and heat water — Chinese Broccoli with Oyster Sauce" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 2. Season the boiling water

Add the olive oil and salt to the boiling water.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/zi3FB2NtLCc?start=92&amp;end=107" title="Season the water — Chinese Broccoli with Oyster Sauce" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 3. Trim and add the gai lan

Trim the stems so they cook evenly. Split any especially thick stems lengthwise. Add the gai lan only after the water is boiling, then cover. Add more boiling water if needed to submerge it.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/zi3FB2NtLCc?start=107&amp;end=151" title="Trim and add gai lan — Chinese Broccoli with Oyster Sauce" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 4. Cook until tender-crisp

Once the water returns to a boil, cook for 1–2 minutes, turning the gai lan about halfway through.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/zi3FB2NtLCc?start=151&amp;end=252" title="Cook the gai lan — Chinese Broccoli with Oyster Sauce" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 5. Check the texture

Uncover the wok and test a stem. If you prefer it softer, cook for another 1–3 minutes.

<div class="recipe-video-frame"><iframe data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/zi3FB2NtLCc?start=252&amp;end=272" title="Check the gai lan — Chinese Broccoli with Oyster Sauce" loading="lazy" data-autoplay-video allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### 6. Plate and serve

Turn off the heat and transfer the gai lan to a plate. Cut the stems and leaves into serving portions, then drizzle with oyster sauce and olive oil to taste.
