## Ingredients

- Pork belly
- Spring onions
- Garlic cloves
- Cooking oil, a small amount
- Light soy sauce, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- White sugar, a small pinch

The source gives no ingredient weights, timings, or serving count. Its photo calls for two spoonfuls of light soy sauce without specifying the spoon size; adjust the seasoning to taste.

## Method

### 1. Prepare the ingredients

Slice the pork belly and garlic cloves thinly. Wash and drain the spring onions, then cut them into short lengths.

### 2. Render the pork belly

Heat a small amount of oil in a wok or frying pan over medium heat. Add the pork belly and stir-fry, allowing the fat to render out.

### 3. Brown and drain

Continue frying until the pork is cooked through and lightly golden, with slightly crisp edges. Carefully spoon out the excess rendered fat, leaving a little in the pan for frying. This keeps the finished dish from being greasy.

### 4. Fry the garlic

Add the sliced garlic to the pork and stir-fry briefly until fragrant, without letting it burn.

### 5. Season the pork

Add the light soy sauce, salt, and a small pinch of sugar. Toss well to coat the pork, adjusting the seasoning to taste.

### 6. Add the spring onions

Turn the heat to high and add the spring onions. Stir-fry quickly, tossing just until they are just tender but still crisp. Remove from the heat immediately; prolonged cooking softens them. Serve hot with steamed rice.
