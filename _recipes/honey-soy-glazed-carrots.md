## Ingredients

- 500 g carrots, peeled
- 8 g salt, for blanching
- Ice water, for cooling
- 60 ml honey
- 30 ml soy sauce
- 7.5 ml red chili flakes, optional
- 28 g butter

## Method

### 1. Blanch the carrots

Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add the carrots and blanch for 3 minutes, then transfer them to ice water to cool. Drain well.

[![Carrots blanching in water.](https://tiffycooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/F43C0313-D986-41AD-9CB1-AE3602F74172-768x1024.jpg)](https://tiffycooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/F43C0313-D986-41AD-9CB1-AE3602F74172-768x1024.jpg)

### 2. Make the glaze

Put the honey, soy sauce, chili flakes if using, and butter in a pan over medium-high heat. Bring to a simmer and cook for 2 minutes.

[![Ingredients for the honey soy glaze.](https://tiffycooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/1A5CF48B-4CE0-4844-BDC8-B434A63ACA12-768x1024.jpg)](https://tiffycooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/1A5CF48B-4CE0-4844-BDC8-B434A63ACA12-768x1024.jpg)

### 3. Glaze the carrots

Add the drained carrots and sauté over high heat for 2 minutes, tossing to coat them evenly in the glaze.
