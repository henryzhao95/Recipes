## Ingredients

- 2 scallions
- 15 g ginger, thinly sliced and julienned
- 1 small bunch cilantro, roughly chopped
- 15 ml light soy sauce
- 1 g salt
- 2 g sugar
- 30 ml hot water
- 1 white fish fillet, about 280 g
- 30 ml vegetable oil

## Method

### 1. Prep the aromatics

Trim the scallions, then cut them into 2-inch pieces and halve them lengthwise. Slice the ginger thinly and julienne it. Roughly chop the cilantro and set everything aside.

[![Prepared scallions, ginger, and cilantro for steamed fish.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-5.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-5.jpg)

### 2. Mix the sauce

Combine the light soy sauce, salt, sugar, and hot water in a small bowl. Stir until the salt and sugar dissolve completely.

[![Soy sauce mixture for steamed fish.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-3.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-3.jpg)

### 3. Set up the steamer

Fill a wok or steamer with 1–2 inches of water and bring it to a boil over high heat.

[![Steaming rack set over water in a wok.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-2.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-2.jpg)

### 4. Prep the fish

Rinse the fish fillet and lay it on a heatproof plate or shallow dish that fits in your steamer. Place it into the steamer and reduce the heat to medium so the water maintains a steady but not violent boil.

[![Fish fillet on a plate going into the steamer.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-4.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-4.jpg)

### 5. Steam the fish

Cover and steam for 7–10 minutes, depending on the thickness of the fillet. It is ready when the flesh flakes easily and a knife can slide through the thickest part without resistance.

[![Checking steamed fish for doneness with a knife.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-6.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-6.jpg)

### 6. Scatter the aromatics

Turn off the heat and carefully drain any liquid from the plate. Sprinkle about one-third of the scallions, ginger, and cilantro over the fish.

[![Draining liquid from the plate of steamed fish.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-7.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-7.jpg)

### 7. Build the hot sauce

Heat the vegetable oil in a wok or small saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the remaining ginger and cook for 1 minute. Add the white parts of the scallions and cook for about 30 seconds, then add the remaining scallions and cilantro. The mixture should be sizzling.

[![Heating oil for the sauce.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-8.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-8.jpg)

[![Adding ginger to the hot oil.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-9.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-9.jpg)

[![Adding the white parts of the scallions.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-10.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-10.jpg)

[![Adding the remaining scallions and cilantro.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-11.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-11.jpg)

### 8. Add the seasoning mixture

Pour in the soy sauce mixture and stir gently. Bring it to a simmer and cook until the scallions and cilantro just begin to wilt, about 30 seconds.

[![Pouring the soy sauce mixture into the pan.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-12.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-12.jpg)

[![Simmering sauce for the fish.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-13.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-13.jpg)

### 9. Finish and serve

Pour the hot sauce over the steamed fish. If you prefer, add the reserved raw aromatics on top just before serving. Serve immediately while the fish is still hot.

[![Pouring the hot sauce over the steamed fish.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-20.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-20.jpg)

[![Finished steamed fish with sauce and aromatics.](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-14.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cantonese-steamed-fish-14.jpg)
