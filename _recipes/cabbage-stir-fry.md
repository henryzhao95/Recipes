## Ingredients

- 15 ml vegetable oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 450 g shredded cabbage
- 15 ml soy sauce
- 15 ml Shaoxing rice wine

## Method

### 1. Fry the garlic

Heat the oil in a wok or large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and stir-fry for 3–5 seconds, just until it begins to brown.

### 2. Cook the cabbage

Add the cabbage and toss to coat in oil. Cover and cook for 1 minute.

### 3. Season and finish

Add the soy sauce and stir-fry for 1 minute. Increase the heat to high, add the rice wine, and cook for about 2 minutes more until the cabbage is tender.

### 4. Serve

Serve hot.
